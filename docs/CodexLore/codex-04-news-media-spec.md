# CODECRAFT — NEWS AGENCIES & MEDIA LORE SPECIFICATION

This document outlines the major regional news outlets, independent reporting networks, and pirate broadcasts operating across the Spokane grid. These agencies serve as primary conduits for world flavor, reactive story updates, and hidden puzzle clues inside SPLICE browser pages and codex logs.

---

## 1. Corporate & Official News Outlets

### S.I.N. (Spokane Information Network)
* **Parent Organization:** Municipal Broadcasters Guild / SMDA Monopoly
* **The Vibe:** The polished, state-sanctioned voice of Spokane. High corporate polish, heavily censored, and aggressively optimistic about corporate initiatives.
* **Biases & Tone:** Covers up grid failures, frames industrial accidents as "routine maintenance," and portrays deckers/anarchists as dangerous infrastructure terrorists.
* **Domain / URL:** `sin-news.com`

#### Visual Page Styling Spec
* **Archetype:** High-Gloss Modern News Portal
* **Palette:** Corporate blue (`#003366`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), bright yellow ticker highlights (`#FFD700`), slate gray cards (`#1E293B`).
* **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif (`Inter` or `Roboto`).
* **Page Elements:** Breaking news ticker banner across the top, video thumbnail placeholders with play icons, official press release embeds, and suppressed comment sections.

#### Headlines & Story Concepts
1. **"SMDA Announces Substation 09 Upgrades; Brief Localized Voltage Fluctuations Expected"**
   * *The Real Story:* A major surge caused by illegal grid-tapping burned out three transformers, brown-out targeting lower-income residential blocks to preserve corporate grid draw.
2. **"Monolith Tactical Reports 14% Drop in Transit Corridor Incidents"**
   * *The Real Story:* Monolith stopped logging incidents in non-corporate zones to artificially inflate safety statistics.
3. **"A.V.I.S.T.A. Reaffirms Commitment to Clean Hydro Energy Following River Intake Inspection"**
   * *The Real Story:* Divers found illegal underwater fiber cables tapped directly into W.W.P. legacy conduits near Riverfront Park.

### Inland Business Journal (I.B.J.)
* **Parent Organization:** Independent Corporate Syndicate
* **The Vibe:** High-finance, cold, analytical news aimed at corporate suits, investors, and high-tier tech executives.
* **Biases & Tone:** Zero concern for human cost or street-level disruption; cares exclusively about stock valuations, merger rumors, tech patent filings, and supply chain logistics.
* **Domain / URL:** `inland-biz.io`

#### Visual Page Styling Spec
* **Archetype:** Financial Data & Market Terminal
* **Palette:** Dark terminal background (`#0D1117`), stock-ticker green (`#238636`), loss red (`#DA3633`), gold accents (`#D4AF37`).
* **Typography:** Monospaced data tables paired with compact serif headers.
* **Page Elements:** Live ticker showing stock prices for Aetheron, Monolith, and Apex; market analytics charts; gated "Subscriber Only" deep-dive reports requiring corporate credentials.

#### Headlines & Story Concepts
1. **"Aetheron Bio-Synthetics Class-B Stock Surges Following Patent Approval on Series-9 Sensory Buses"**
   * *The Real Story:* The patent forces immediate firmware deprecation on Series-7 models, driving street patients to illegal back-alley Cyber Docs.
2. **"C.O.P.P.E.R.H.E.A.D. Supply Disruptions Drive Heavy Steel Futures Up 8%"**
   * *The Real Story:* A freight raid in the Valley scrap corridors held up a major shipment of mil-spec chassis parts.
3. **"P.R.O.V.I.D.E.N.C.E. Acquires University District Bio-Research Annex in $42M Expansion"**
   * *The Real Story:* A quiet land grab to buy out subterranean research space previously linked to early G.O.N.Z.A.G.A. neural experiments.

## 2. Independent & Underground News Outlets

### The Valley Voice (Independent Local Gazette)
* **Parent Organization:** Independent Civilian Collective (Browne's Addition / Valley)
* **The Vibe:** Gritty, boots-on-the-ground neighborhood journalism. Written by weary locals for working-class citizens, street techs, and shop owners.
* **Biases & Tone:** Highly critical of corporate monopolies, sympathetic to street-level struggles, but skeptical of radical hacker groups who knock out neighborhood power.
* **Domain / URL:** `valley-voice.org`

#### Visual Page Styling Spec
* **Archetype:** Minimalist Digital Newspaper / Independent Blog
* **Palette:** Paper tan background (`#EFECE6`), dark charcoal ink text (`#1A1A1A`), warm brick red accents (`#A63A2A`).
* **Typography:** Serif body text (`Merriweather` or `Georgia`) with bold headline fonts.
* **Page Elements:** Community message board, localized outage maps submitted by users, "Lost & Stolen Hardware" classifieds.

#### Headlines & Story Concepts
1. **"Rolling Blackouts Hit Hillyard for Third Straight Night: Residents Demand Answers from A.V.I.S.T.A."**
   * *The Real Story:* Power is being secretly diverted to a subterranean server installation operating below the industrial district.
2. **"Beware the Alley Docs: Three Unregistered Cyber Implants Fail in Browne's Addition"**
   * *The Real Story:* S.T.I.T.C.H.E.R.S. operators are dumping faulty second-hand neural buses on the black market, forcing Patch to clean up the aftermath.
3. **"Mysterious Frequency Interference Disrupts Local Shortwave Radios Near Monroe Street Bridge"**
   * *The Real Story:* Early symptoms of a Splice Frequency resonance test leak from a nearby forgotten node.

### WIRE-DEAD (Pirate Data Dump & Rogue Feed)
* **Parent Organization:** Unaligned Decker Cells (Associated with N.U.L.L. underground)
* **The Vibe:** Aggressive, unfiltered pirate broadcast and raw data leak network. No editors, no corporate filter, pure signal.
* **Biases & Tone:** Anti-corporate, radical transparency, chaos-leaning. Often drops raw raw terminal logs, unredacted corporate emails, and leaked audio clips.
* **Domain / URL:** `wire-dead.net` (or `wiredead.onion`)

#### Visual Page Styling Spec
* **Archetype:** CRT Terminal Leak Site / Underground Feed
* **Palette:** Deep pitch black (`#050505`), high-phosphor amber (`#FFB000`) or glitch cyan (`#00F0FF`), scanline overlay effect.
* **Typography:** Monospaced CRT terminal font (`VT323` or `Share Tech Mono`).
* **Page Elements:** Audio waveform player for leaked communications, "Raw File Downloads" (`.txt`, `.log`), live terminal leak stream updating in real time.

#### Headlines & Story Concepts
1. **"[LEAK] Internal Monolith Patrol Schedule for Sprague Corridor (08.12 - 08.19)"**
   * *Game Use:* Players can use these leaked schedules to find windows of low security enforcement in specific districts.
2. **"[AUDIO TRANSCRIPT] Intercepted Apex Infrastructure High-Voltage Line Feed"**
   * *Game Use:* Audio log of two engineers discovering an unauthorized signal piggybacking on an active power trunk.
3. **"EXPOSED: P.R.O.V.I.D.E.N.C.E. Bio-Debt Repossession Protocols Revealed"**
   * *Game Use:* A dark-web document explaining how corporate repossession units track deactivated neural implants across the city.
