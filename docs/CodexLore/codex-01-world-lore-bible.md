# CODEX PAGE 1 — REVISED MASTER WORLD LORE BIBLE

* **Type:** Core Codex / Master Lore Overview
* **Overview:** Defines the primary power structures, regional history, and faction dynamics governing the Spokane SPLICE network.

---

### 1. Regional Infrastructure & Corporate Monopolies
* **A.V.I.S.T.A. (Alpine Valley Integrated Substation & Transmission Authority):** Controls the municipal power grid, hydro-dams along the Spokane River, and high-draw industrial power conduits. Aggressively throttles bandwidth and power in working-class districts (e.g., Hillyard/Valley) to divert current to corporate nodes.
* **P.R.O.V.I.D.E.N.C.E. (Primary Rescriptive Organic & Vital Implant Diagnostics Enclave):** Monopolizes high-tier trauma care, aug-rejection therapy, and bio-synthetic organ replacement[cite: 1]. Enforces forced firmware deprecation on legacy hardware (e.g., Series-7 buses), driving patients to back-alley Cyber Docs like Patch[cite: 1].
* **G.O.N.Z.A.G.A. (Global Optical & Neural Zero-point Architecture Graduate Academy):** Elite riverfront research institute. Ground zero for early corporate-funded "Persistence" frequency tests conducted in secret subterranean labs before a major containment breach.

### 2. Underground Syndicates & Street Factions
* **C.O.P.P.E.R.H.E.A.D.:** Heavy-rig modders, physical hardware smugglers, and chassis chop shops operating out of the Spokane Valley rail corridors[cite: 1, 3]. Knuckle's primary source for structural steel[cite: 1, 3].
* **I.N.L.A.N.D.:** Dominant street protection syndicate running digital extortion, protection taxes on illegal nodes, and illicit card dens[cite: 3]. Fronts as a legitimate commercial leasing firm[cite: 3].
* **S.T.I.T.C.H.E.R.S.:** Desperate alleyway organ-harvesters and unlicensed cyberware butchers operating out of dark basement bays[cite: 1]. Direct rival to Patch[cite: 1].
* **N.U.L.L.:** Radical frequency squatters, signal anarchists, and code purists fighting for an unmonitored SPLICE network[cite: 3].
* **S.P.E.C.T.R.E.:** Anti-augmentation militia squatters who view the SPLICE network as "neural poisoning" and deploy crude EMP cannons against municipal relays.
